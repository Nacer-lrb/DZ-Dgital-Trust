# Legal context and sources

[Handbook](README.md) · [Français](../fr/legal.md) · [العربية](../ar/legal.md)

Law 26-02 of 17 February 2026 provides the project's reference framework for trust services and electronic identification. Its transitional provisions require a distinction between the intended framework and its effective implementation. The project does not certify that this transition is complete.

## Official documents

- [Law 26-02, Official Journal 14 of 2026](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2026/F2026014.pdf): articles 2, 4–12, 47–52 and 109–115 are used in the study.
- [Law 15-04, Official Journal 6 of 2015](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2015/F2015006.pdf): previous framework, to be read with the transitional provisions.
- [Law 18-07, Official Journal 34 of 2018](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2018/F2018034.pdf), amended by [Law 25-11, Official Journal 48 of 2025](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2025/F2025048.pdf): personal data.

The local PDF contains the full Official Journal issue, not only Law 26-02. French editions identify themselves as translations. The [register](../../resources/references.md) records sources and outstanding research.

## Documentation scope

The French, English and Arabic handbooks explain the project; they are not official translations of legislation. The [detailed analysis](../algeria/law-26-02-analysis.md), [institutional study](../algeria/institutions.md) and [timeline](../algeria/legal-timeline.md) are French reference studies.

A cryptographic result or a PAdES profile alone does not establish a qualified signature. The demonstration verifies no real identity and provides no accredited service. Applicable implementing regulations and published policies must be examined before real integration.
